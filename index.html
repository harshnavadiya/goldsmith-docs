Set up Goldsmith

Goldsmith is a Shopify theme for jewelers who sell by weight and by stone. Each price can show what it's made of: the gold at your rate, the stones and the making charge. Loose diamonds come with their grading report and a link to check it on the lab's own website.

  • Built for gold and diamond jewelry
  • Catalog small to medium
  • Setup theme editor and product fields, no code

Getting started

  1. Add the theme. In Online Store > Themes, add Goldsmith, then choose Customize.
  2. Enter your gold rates in Theme settings > Gold rates. See Gold rates.
  3. Give each piece its gold weight and karat as product fields. See Product data.
  4. Choose your look in Theme settings > Colors and Typography.
  5. Build your home page from the sections listed in Sections and templates.

Every part that depends on your data hides itself until the data is there, so the theme looks complete from the first install.

Gold rates

In Theme settings > Gold rates, enter your rate per gram for 24k gold. Enter 22k, 18k and 14k too if you quote them separately. Left empty, each is worked out from the 24k rate by purity (22k is 22/24 of it, and so on).

Fill in Rates as of with the date you set them, so customers know how current they are. The date shows everywhere a rate does:

  • the rate bar above the header;
  • karat links in the main menu;
  • the footer's gold-per-gram table;
  • price breakdowns, the budget calculator and the home page price example.

The theme never changes your product prices. Prices stay what you set in the admin. If you want prices to follow a live gold rate automatically, use a pricing app; the theme works fully without one.

Product data

The theme reads jewelry and diamond details from product metafields. Create them in Settings > Custom data > Products with the namespaces and keys below, and the theme picks them up automatically. Already use other field names? Change the namespaces in Theme settings > Product data, or connect each detail on the diamond page to any field with Connect dynamic source.

Price breakdowns

When a piece has a gold weight and karat and you've entered gold rates, the theme shows what its price is made of:

  • Gold is the weight times your rate for that karat.
  • A piece with stones: the making charge comes from the piece's own making charge, and the stones are the rest of the price. Customers see the stones at their price per carat, so they can compare them with loose stones.
  • A piece without stones: the making charge is the rest of the price, shown as a percent of the gold value.
How it looks for a 14k solitaire at a $79.90/g rate. The rows always add up to the price you set.
Gold2.90 g × $79.90/g · 14k$231.71
Stones (lab-grown)0.30 ct SI1/H at $504.00/ct$151.20
Making charge12% of the gold value$27.81
The price you pay$410.72

If the numbers can't add up (for example, the gold alone would cost more than the price), the breakdown stays hidden rather than show figures that don't match.

Ring sizes

A ring (a product tagged ring) opens on its first available size, with a Change size link beside the price. To make customers choose a size before adding to cart, turn on Ask for a ring size before adding to cart in the product section.

Diamonds

Diamond page

Assign the product.diamond template to loose diamonds. It shows:

  • the price and the price per carat;
  • the grading report: lab, report number with a Copy button, date and inscription;
  • a button that opens the report on the lab's own website;
  • every grade with what it means, including "Not graded" for cut on shapes labs don't grade;
  • the stone's photos, its 360° video (loaded only when the customer asks), or a labeled line drawing of its shape.

Report links

Theme settings > Grading labs holds each lab's report-check address. {number} is replaced by the stone's report number. GIA, IGI, HRD and GCAL are filled in, and you can add one more lab. A lab's name must match the stone's lab value, in any capitalization.

Diamond search

Assign the collection.diamonds template to a collection of loose diamonds. Customers can:

  • filter by shape, carat, price, color, clarity, cut, lab, and natural or lab-grown;
  • see the filters in use as chips, and remove any of them in one tap;
  • compare stones by price per carat, with each stone's origin in its own column;
  • sort by price, price per carat, carat, color or clarity, from the Sort menu or by pressing a column heading;
  • share a search by its address.

The search lists up to 250 stones on one page. For bigger catalogs, use the standard collection template with filters from the Shopify Search and Discovery app.

Similar stones

Under each diamond, the Similar stones section shows other stones from your diamonds collection: the same origin only, within a carat range of the stone (50% either way by default), with the same shape and closest weight first. Choose another collection or range in the section's settings. If no stone fits, the section doesn't show.

Product page

Blocks you can reorder or remove: title, vendor, price, variant picker, quantity, buy buttons, price breakdown, specifications, center stone report, description, collapsible tabs, text, Custom Liquid and app blocks.

The gallery shows images, videos, YouTube and Vimeo, and 3D models with "View in your space". Variant images switch when a variant is chosen. Product recommendations can show related or complementary pieces.

Other templates

Collection and search (with filters and sorting), all collections, blog, article, contact, cart, 404, password and gift card.

Colors and fonts

Theme settings > Colors holds four color schemes; each section picks one. The first is white with steel ink, the second a light panel, the third deep steel for a band, and the fourth a warm gold wash. Edit any of them, or add more.

Theme settings > Typography sets the heading and body fonts from Shopify's font library.

Theme settings > Social media takes your profile links for the icons in the footer.

Shopify features

  • Dynamic checkout buttons on the product page and cart.
  • Shop Pay Installments under the buy buttons, when your store offers it.
  • Follow on Shop in the footer, when Shop Pay is on. You can hide it in the footer section.
  • Country and language selectors in the header and footer, once you sell in more than one market or language.
  • Pickup availability on the product page.
  • Subscriptions (selling plans) on the product page and in the cart.
  • Gift cards with a recipient form, and a gift card page with a QR code and Apple Wallet.
  • Unit prices on the product page, product cards and cart.
  • Link previews and search results: shared links show the page's title and image, and products carry Google's product details.

Questions

The rate bar and price breakdowns don't appear.
Enter at least a 24k rate in Theme settings > Gold rates, and give the piece a gold weight and karat.
One piece's breakdown is hidden.
The gold at your rate would cost more than the piece's price, or the piece has stones but its price leaves nothing for them. Check its weight, karat and your rates.
Can I sell in more than one currency?
Yes. The country selector appears once you have more than one market. Rates and breakdowns are worked out in your store's currency and shown converted, marked "≈", with a note.
The report button opens the wrong page.
Check the lab's address in Theme settings > Grading labs. The lab's name there must match the stone's lab value.
Where do the diamond outline drawings come from?
The theme draws round, oval, princess, cushion, emerald, pear, marquise, radiant, asscher and heart shapes. A shape entry with an icon image uses that image instead.
What shows when someone shares a link to my store?
The page's title, description and image: the product's, collection's or article's own image, or your store's social sharing image from Online Store > Preferences.
Where's the Follow on Shop button?
In the footer, when Shop Pay is turned on for your store.
Does the theme change my prices when I change the rates?
No. Prices are always the ones you set. The rates explain them; a pricing app can keep prices in step with a live rate if you want that.

Editing code

Everything in this guide is set up in the theme editor and the Shopify admin. If you edit the theme's code, duplicate the theme first (Online Store > Themes > ⋯ > Duplicate) so you can go back. Code changes aren't covered by theme support; for custom work, we suggest hiring a Shopify Partner.

Support

Questions, or something not working as described? Contact us through the support form. Include your store address (for example storename.myshopify.com), what you expected to happen, and a screenshot if you can.
